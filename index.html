Why these parts

  • Processor: 16 of AMD's newer Zen 5 cores, about 12% faster on full sweeps than the older 7950X. Sweeps run 1 case per core, so cores matter most. Bought used from CeX, it costs about £62 less than new.
  • RAM: a factory-matched pair of 2 x 16 GB. 2 sticks run at full speed on this platform, and 4 sticks would run slower. A second matching pair later gives 64 GB.
  • Motherboard: good build quality and power delivery for a 16-core chip. Its 2 USB-C ports carry video, so it runs 3 screens with no graphics card. It also takes newer AM5 chips, expected in 2027.
  • Cooler: a dual-tower air cooler rated for 280 W. It has no pump to fail and is simple to fit.
  • Power supply: 850 W Gold with a 10-year warranty. The PC peaks at about 310 W, which leaves room for a gaming card later.
  • Case and fans: a high-airflow case with 4 fans included, 3 of them RGB, plus 2 RGB fans on top for long sweeps. The RGB cooler matches them.
  • No graphics card yet: the built-in graphics use about 1 to 2 GB of RAM. If RAM fills up during sweeps, a used card can be added in minutes.

Build and setup notes

Fan layout: the case's 3 RGB fans at the front blow air in. The 2 RGB Arctic fans on top and the rear case fan blow air out. All 6 plug into the case hub, which takes 1 fan header and 1 RGB header on the board. The cooler's RGB plugs into a second RGB header.

Build order

  1. Fit the processor, RAM, SSD and cooler on the motherboard while it sits on its box. Keep the socket cover on until the processor goes in, and drop the chip in without sliding it.
  2. Put the RAM in slots A2 and B2, the second and fourth slots from the processor.
  3. Plug in the processor power cables at the top of the board before fitting the top fans.
  4. Put the SSD in the top M.2 slot.
  5. On first start, wait at least 5 minutes. The board tests the RAM and can show a black screen meanwhile.

BIOS settings

  • Turn on EXPO for the RAM. Until then it runs at a slower default speed, usually 4800 or 5600. EXPO is a factory-tested overclock profile that sets 6400 CL32 at 1.35 V in one click. If it is not stable at 6400, set it to 6000, which is just as good in practice.
  • Set UMA Frame Buffer Size to 512 MB, so the screens use as little RAM as possible.
  • Set a quiet fan curve.

Day one checks: run MemTest86 overnight, and check the SSD's health with CrystalDiskInfo. Do this while the CeX returns window is still open.

Upgrades later: a second matching 2 x 16 GB kit for 64 GB, a graphics card if RAM is tight or for gaming, and a newer AM5 processor.
